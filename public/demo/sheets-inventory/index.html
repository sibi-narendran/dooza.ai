How this maps to Google Sheets

Copy as Google Sheets paste puts tab-separated rows on your clipboard. Click cell A1 in a blank sheet and paste: every column lands in its own cell.
  1. Items tab: SKU | Name | Location | Qty | Reorder point | Unit cost | Stock value | Status. Stock value is =D2*F2, status is =IF(D2=0,"OUT",IF(D2<=E2,"REORDER","OK")).
  2. Stock Log tab: Date | SKU | Item | Type | Qty | Note. One row per move, never edited, so you keep a full history.
  3. Qty on hand can come from the log instead of being typed: =SUMIFS(Log!E:E,Log!B:B,A2,Log!D:D,"in")-SUMIFS(Log!E:E,Log!B:B,A2,Log!D:D,"out") plus the opening count.
  4. Dashboard tab: total value =SUM(Items!G:G), low-stock count =COUNTIF(Items!H:H,"<>OK"), last 10 moves with =SORT(Log!A2:F,1,FALSE).
  5. Apps Script (Extensions, Apps Script): a stock in/out form in a sidebar that appends to the log, an onEdit trigger that recolors low rows, and a daily time trigger that emails the reorder list to you.

The same screens can live inside your Google Sheet, or stay a web app like this one that reads and writes the Sheet.
