    Outreach scripts for a venue owner

    Short, warm, one ask each. Replace the [brackets]. Mention the specific thing you noticed on their site, so it never reads like a mass mail.

    1. First email

    Subject: A free listing for [Venue name] before we launch

Hi [Name],

I came across [Venue name] while looking for [area] venues that host [Asian / Jewish / Nigerian / Greek...] weddings, and [one specific detail from their site] really stood out.

We're launching [Platform], a wedding-planning platform for London couples, and we're inviting a small group of venues to be listed before launch. It's free. Couples search by the things that matter to them: guest numbers, catering rules, parking, late finishes, and whether you're used to multi-day or religious ceremonies.

Would you have 10 minutes this week for a quick call? I'll set your profile up for you while we talk. If you'd rather do it yourself, it's here: [your sign-up link]

Warm regards,
[Your name]
[Platform], London

    2. WhatsApp opener

    Only where the venue lists WhatsApp for enquiries on its own site or profile.

    Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from [Platform], a new wedding-planning site for London couples. I loved [specific detail] on your page. We're listing a few [area] venues free before launch, and I can set your profile up for you in 10 minutes. Would a quick call tomorrow or Thursday suit you? If now's not a good time, no problem at all.

    3. Phone opener

    "Hi, is that [Name]? It's [Your name] from [Platform]. Have I caught you at an okay moment?

(If yes) I'll be quick. We're a new wedding-planning platform for London couples, and we're adding a small group of venues before launch, free of charge. [Venue] came up because you host a lot of [community] weddings in [area].

I'd love 10 minutes with you to set up your profile properly: capacity, catering rules, parking, finishing time. That way the couples who get in touch are actually a fit for you. Would later this week work, say Wednesday or Thursday?"

(If busy) "No problem at all. When's a better time to call you back?"

    Common objections and short answers

    "Another directory?"Fair question. Most directories charge you to be seen and then send you every enquiry going. We match couples on what you tell us (guest numbers, catering rules, dates, the ceremonies you host), so you get fewer enquiries that fit better. And listing is free while we launch.
    "What does it cost?"Nothing to be listed. [If paid options come later, say exactly what and when.] I'd rather you see real enquiries come in first, then decide.
    "We're fully booked."That's great to hear. Lots of couples book a year or more ahead, so a profile now helps fill your next season. Shall I set it up now and check in with you in [month]?
    "Send me info."Of course. I'll email a one-page summary and a sample listing today. Could I call you on [day] for 5 minutes once you've had a look?

    Cultural and religious sensitivity notes

    • Ask, don't assume: "What kinds of weddings do you host most?" works better than naming a community you've guessed.
    • Timing: avoid calling Jewish-owned businesses on Shabbat (Friday sunset to Saturday night) and on major festivals; avoid prayer and iftar times in Ramadan; expect South Asian vendors to be at their busiest around Diwali and peak wedding season.
    • Capture the details couples from each community search for: halal or kosher kitchens, own-caterer policy, alcohol-free packages, space for a mandap, chuppah, tea ceremony or multi-day events, live fire and dhol rules, late finishes.
    • Use the vendor's own words for what they do (for example "Asian wedding specialist", "kosher caterer") in the listing.
