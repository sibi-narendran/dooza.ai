Exact Workflow to Deliver 150 Verified Contacts (100 NYC + 50 Westchester)

1. Newly Built / Unleased ~60-Unit NYC Buildings

Query NYC DOB Certificate of Occupancy (bs8b-p36w) and DOB NOW: Build Job Filings (w9ak-ipjd) for New Building (NB) and Major Alteration (Alt-1) properties with 48–72 dwelling units issued a TCO or Final CO in the last 90 days (e.g., 408 Lefferts Ave — 60 units, CO issued Oct 26, 2026; 284 Humboldt St — 64 units, CO issued Oct 29, 2026) before retail lease-up finishes.

2. Stalled Construction & Vacant Multifamily

Cross-reference NYC DOB Stalled Construction Sites, expired NB permits at 60%+ completion, and NYC DOF RPIE / HPD Vacate & Major Renovation filings to catch developers carrying high construction-loan debt who welcome a single creditworthy supportive-housing master tenant for all ~60 units.

3. Piercing Anonymous LLCs to Reach Real Decision-Makers

Every NYC multifamily LLC must file an individual Head Officer and Managing Agent in NYC HPD Multiple Dwelling Registrations (feu5-w2e2) + ACRIS mortgage signatory pages. We skip-trace direct phone + verified email for the named principal before dialing.

4. Westchester ~20-Unit Transit-Accessible Properties

Filter the NY State Assessment Roll (7vem-aaz7, Property Class 411 Apartments) + municipal planning board NB approvals in Yonkers, New Rochelle, Mount Vernon, White Plains, and Peekskill within 0.6 miles of Metro-North & grocery corridors, targeting $3.5M–$7.5M buildings (~16–26 units).
