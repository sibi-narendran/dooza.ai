How do I use retinol safely?
  • Patch test first. Apply a small amount behind the ear or on the inner arm and wait 24 to 48 hours.
  • Start slowly. Use a pea-sized amount two or three evenings a week, then build up as your skin gets used to it.
  • Evenings only. Apply to clean, dry skin, then follow with moisturiser.
  • Wear SPF every day. Retinol can make skin more sensitive to the sun.
  • Don't double up. Avoid using it on the same night as glycolic acid or other exfoliants.
  • Some dryness or tingling at first is common. Stop if irritation persists. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding or under a doctor's care, ask your doctor before use.
Can I use glycolic acid and vitamin C together?

We suggest vitamin C in the morning and glycolic acid in the evening, on different nights from retinol. Always wear SPF during the day when using exfoliating acids.

How do I get an even self tan?

Exfoliate and dry off first. Apply the Tanning Mousse with a mitt in circular motions, go lightly on knees, elbows and ankles, then wash your hands. For the Tanning Drops, mix a few drops into your moisturiser and blend evenly. Self tanner does not protect against the sun, so keep wearing SPF.
