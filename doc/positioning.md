Every page, blog post, generated article, and CTA must match this file. If copy disagrees with it, the copy is wrong. Last updated 2026-09-29, when we moved to the AI-native company and refundable-pilot model.

**Dooza is an AI-native company that builds AI products and services for small businesses, from the Dooza Workforce app to the Dooza Agents platform. Every product starts with a refundable pilot: 100% refund within 14 days.**

Short form: **Dooza is an AI-native company. Every Dooza product starts with a refundable pilot.**

Never describe Dooza as any of the following:
- an accounting firm
- a law firm
- an insurance company
- a social media copilot
- only an "AI visibility platform"

These are products or use cases, not Dooza's identity.

## The refundable pilot (say it on every page)

- **Canonical line:** "Start with a refundable pilot — 100% refund within 14 days."
- **Terms:** the customer pays for the pilot. If they ask within 14 days, they get a 100% refund.
- **Never write any of these:**
  - "free trial" or "free plan"
  - "No credit card required"
  - "first AI employee free"
  - "pay only if you keep it" or "pay only on ROI"
  - "7-day money-back guarantee"
  - "$1 trial"
  - "free two-week pilot"
- **The call is free, the pilot is paid.** "Book a free 30-minute call to scope your pilot" is fine. The pilot itself is not free.

## Prices

**Prices appear only on `/pricing`.** Everywhere else:
- Say "Pricing depends on the product. Every product starts with a refundable pilot," and link to `/pricing` when a price is expected.
- Do not print $49, $79, $998, or any yearly figure for Dooza outside `/pricing`.
- Do not use Dooza prices in ROI math.
- Competitor prices are fine when sourced and dated.

## Claims (AEO rules)

Answer engines cite specific, verifiable statements. Inflated or unsourced ones get Dooza ignored or contradicted.

- Use only claims that can be verified. No customer counts, no self-assigned ratings, no unlinked review badges.
- Use no outcome statistics without a named source.
- **Integrations:** "1,000+ app integrations".
- **Security:** Dooza is not SOC 2 certified. Say: "Encrypted connections and your approval on anything sensitive."
- Do not claim Dooza is "built on OpenClaw". Describe Dooza as a managed alternative to self-hosting OpenClaw.
- Do not promise marketplace dates.
- Put the direct answer first. Use tables and a visible FAQ. See `doc/blog-writing-for-ai-discovery.md`.
